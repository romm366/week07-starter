# Fictional equipment-loan practice codebook

Counting unit: distinct new W7 encounter IDs per code and method. Interview and observation samples are unlinked. Every record is fictional. Binary coding covers all ten new records and both codes; there are no uncertain/excluded cases in this worked example. S1/S2 are background, not extra rows or denominators.

- access_constraint: include a reported inability to collect within opening hours, or a visibly unsuccessful door entry outside posted hours. This operational code groups related access evidence, not identical intentions. Positive IDs: I1, I3, O2. Exclude missing student ID (I8), which is a different barrier, and do not infer O2's intended task.
- equipment_unavailable: include an interview account that a requested item was unavailable at an attempted collection, or an observed encounter in which staff reported no requested item available and no item was supplied. Positive IDs: I4, O1. Exclude mere uncertainty about availability (I5); an unclear page is not verified item unavailability.

Zero means the record does not meet this definition. It does not prove a person never experiences the issue. Codes can overlap. O1 establishes a reported shortage and no loan in that encounter, not an audited stock level or the cause of a shortage. These two codes illustrate counting; they do not cover every meaningful theme. Keep identification, information uncertainty and successful loans visible in qualitative findings.

Expected counts: access_constraint interview 2/8, observation 1/2; equipment_unavailable interview 1/8, observation 1/2. These fractions describe this fictional dataset only. They do not establish the most common barrier on campus or permit pooling with W4's twenty requests.
